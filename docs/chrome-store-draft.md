## Name
Recall

## Short description (max 132 characters)
Right-click anything, say what it is and why you love it. Saved to your Recall library.

## Single-purpose statement
Recall saves the page, image, link or selected text you are looking at to your own Recall library, together with a spoken or typed note.

## Permission reasons
- **contextMenus:** adds "Add to Recall" to the right-click menu.
- **activeTab:** reads the page you click on (its address, title, selected text or image) only when you choose Add to Recall.
- **scripting:** grabs the selected text or image from that page at that moment.
- **storage:** remembers your small settings in this browser.
- **Host access, recall.nextmissionsi.com:** sends what you save to your Recall library and uses your Recall sign-in.
- **Microphone (asked once by Chrome):** lets you speak your note.
