# Roadmap

What's built, and what's next, in order. The why behind each step is in [STORY.md](STORY.md).

## Built

- Save anything from an iPhone (share sheet, voice), a Mac (⌃⌥S, Finder, the Save page) or Chrome (right-click), with one spoken sentence: what it is, what it's for, why you love it.
- A local librarian that reads, describes and files each save into a project, and asks one question in Clarify when it can't tell.
- Find by description; projects as boards; topics that form, divide and merge on their own.
- Reminders from plain words ("bring this back next Friday") and "brought back for you".
- Private by default: one library per person, Google sign-in, invite links, a team link.
- Dream boards: share projects with a team, live, with everyone's name and face.
- Creator mode: pictures and words from a board into a new image or video.
- Long documents, meetings and voice notes read in full and split into pieces, each sorted on its own and linked back to its spot.

## Next

1. **An always-on server**, so Recall doesn't depend on one laptop being awake ([docs/server.md](docs/server.md)).
2. **A welcome page and a click-by-click manual** for people opening Recall for the first time.
3. **Connectors that sync on their own:** pictures from a "Recall" album, Apple Notes, meeting recorders (Otter, Granola), Google Docs and Drive, Claude, voice notes.
4. **Sharing:** send a project to a friend as a read-only board; make a short video of any board.
5. **Sparks:** the highest-inspiration passages from years of notes, pulled out in the writer's own words and brought back on a widening rhythm.
6. **Agents** that act on the pieces: ideas, actions and decisions sent where they need to go.

Ideas and requests: open an issue.
