# Roadmap

What's built, and what's next, in order. The why behind each step is in [STORY.md](STORY.md).

## Built

- Save anything from an iPhone (share sheet, voice), a Mac (⌃⌥S, Finder, the Save page) or Chrome (right-click), with one spoken sentence: what it is, what it's for, why you love it.
- A local librarian that reads, describes and files each save into a project, and asks one question in Clarify when it can't tell.
- Find by description; projects as boards; topics that form, divide and merge on their own.
- Reminders from plain words ("bring this back next Friday") and "brought back for you".
- Private by default: one library per person, Google sign-in, invite links, a team link.
- Dream boards: share projects with a team, live, with everyone's name and face.
- Creator mode: pictures and words from a board into a new image or video.
- Long documents, meetings and voice notes read in full and split into pieces, each sorted on its own and linked back to its spot.

## Built, first version (23 Nov, on the twin, being refined)

- **An always-on server** and **a welcome page with a click-by-click manual.**
- **Three tabs inside every project, one at a time:** **Share** (to a person or their agent), **Create** (pick assets, say how and why; a board video, a reel or post pictures are made in a queue), **Remind** (put an asset into Remind with a note on how, when and what for).
- **Sparks:** older notes are read slowly for the lines worth bringing back, in the writer's own words, on Remind's widening rhythm.
- **Agents** that act on the pieces: a reminder, a save, a meeting's idea or a typed directive goes to an agent. It researches, checks feasibility, finds people and drafts messages, then stops at the first judgment point and asks. It never sends anything.
- **Today:** your way of being for the day, your current work, and what is waiting on you.
- **Connectors page:** every source and destination in one place, with what each one needs.

## Next

1. **Connectors that sync on their own:** Apple Notes, Google Drive, a "Recall" photo album. Meeting recorders (Otter, Granola) already do.
2. **Create, further:** silence and filler cuts, subtitles and overlays on a reel; scheduling drafts to Instagram and LinkedIn; Canva.
3. **Agents, further:** act after your answer (a calendar block, a sent message with your yes), agents on a local model, and voice.
4. **Today, further:** the calendar and life orientation.

## Vision: one line, a whole agent ecosystem (27 Nov 2026)

Where all of this is heading. Saved word for word in Recall under the Max OS project.

- **One line to install.** Anyone pastes a single line into Claude (Claude Code desktop, ideally even their phone). Their Claude reads the whole system, works out which parts to set up, and sets them up on their behalf.
- **Land and expand.** It starts small and unfolds into a full agentic ecosystem built around that person's own Claude, like a car that turns into a giant autonomous robot.
- **Spreads by a line of text.** People share it by sending a friend the line: "Hey, install this."
- **Free to install.** For every connector, Claude uses the computer to walk the person to the page where all they do is click "Create API key", then sends the keys in.
- **They choose what to pay for.** Possible ways Max earns: referrals for installs, a setup fee, a monthly subscription, and a share of the value the system creates, measured over time by a secure mechanism inside the system that tracks the revenue and value it generates.
